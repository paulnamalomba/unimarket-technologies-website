# Unimarket Technologies Brand and UX Prototype Brief

## Project

Create a responsive public website for **Unimarket Technologies**, a technology startup based in Malawi. The website should generate qualified enquiries, explain the company’s capabilities clearly, establish trust, and create a reusable visual foundation for later product interfaces.

Approved logo:

```text
public/logos/logo-design-1-no-bg.png
```

## Company position

Unimarket Technologies is a practical end-to-end technology partner. It helps organizations move from business challenge to designed workflow, implemented system, deployment, and support.

Core offerings:

1. Business solutions and digital transformation.
2. POS software for sales, inventory, reporting, and operations.
3. Card machines/POS terminals and payment workflows.
4. Malawi Revenue Authority Electronic Invoicing System (MRA EIS) solutions.
5. Enterprise software and custom operational platforms.
6. UX/UI research, design systems, wireframes, and interactive prototypes.
7. Responsive website design and development.
8. Full-stack web application design, engineering, integration, and support.

Do not imply official MRA accreditation, named payment partnerships, client counts, certifications, or quantified outcomes unless supplied and verified.

## Audiences

- Small and medium businesses that need reliable sales, stock, payment, and reporting tools.
- Retail and hospitality operators exploring POS and card-machine solutions.
- Finance and operations teams preparing for electronic invoicing workflows.
- Enterprises replacing spreadsheets or disconnected legacy systems.
- Founders and organizations needing UX/UI, websites, or custom applications.
- Procurement and executive stakeholders evaluating delivery credibility and support.

## User goals

- Quickly understand what Unimarket Technologies does.
- Find the right solution by business problem or service.
- Understand a credible delivery process.
- See proof of capability without hype.
- Request a consultation, demo, or quote.
- Reach the company by form, email, phone, or WhatsApp when verified details are available.

## Business goals

- Generate consultation and demo enquiries.
- Make POS and MRA EIS capabilities easy to discover.
- Present one coherent company across hardware, software, and design services.
- Build trust through clear process, transparent scope, and real work.
- Create patterns reusable in future product dashboards and portals.

## Brand direction

Use Inter and the logo-derived blue palette from `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`. The visual language is clean, spacious, precise, and quietly ambitious. Combine white/cool-neutral canvases with deep blue sections, fine circuit/grid motifs, restrained product UI imagery, rounded geometry, and subtle blue depth.

Avoid generic neon cyberpunk, excessive glassmorphism, giant gradients, floating 3D blobs, over-rounded pill controls, and generic stock imagery of holograms.

## Home page content

### Header

Full logo, primary navigation, and “Request a consultation” CTA. Sticky behavior; accessible desktop dropdowns and mobile sheet.

### Hero

Eyebrow: “Business technology, designed to work.”

Headline direction: “Connect your business to better systems.”

Supporting copy: Unimarket Technologies designs and delivers POS, electronic invoicing, enterprise software, websites, and full-stack digital products built around real operational needs.

Primary CTA: “Request a consultation”.

Secondary CTA: “Explore solutions”.

Visual: a refined composition combining a POS/payment terminal, invoice/reporting UI, and responsive dashboard—not an unsupported product screenshot presented as live.

### Trust strip

Use categories such as “Strategy”, “Design”, “Engineering”, “Integration”, and “Support” until verified client/partner evidence exists.

### Solutions

Cards for POS software, card machines, MRA EIS, enterprise software, and business solutions. Each card starts with a business outcome and includes a concise explanation.

### Services

UX/UI design, website development, and full-stack applications. Show tangible deliverables and link to detail pages.

### Delivery process

Discover → Define → Design → Build → Integrate → Support.

Explain what the client gains at each stage.

### Featured solution

A larger POS + MRA EIS section showing a possible flow:

```text
Sale → Payment → Inventory update → Invoice/receipt → Reporting
```

Label any unimplemented or conceptual UI as a concept.

### Proof

Use real case studies when supplied. Until then, show capability examples clearly marked “Example engagement” without invented organizations or results.

### Final CTA

“Let’s design the system your business needs next.”

## Key prototype flows

1. Visitor lands on Home and opens a solution from the mega menu.
2. Retail operator explores POS, compares software and card-machine capabilities, then requests a demo.
3. Finance/operations stakeholder explores MRA EIS, reads process/compliance caveats, and requests a consultation.
4. Founder explores UX/UI or full-stack services, reviews the delivery process/work examples, and submits an enquiry.
5. Mobile visitor opens the navigation sheet, finds Contact, completes the form, and sees success/error states.

## Required components and states

- Header, dropdown/mega menu, mobile sheet, breadcrumbs.
- Hero, logo/trust strip, service cards, solution cards, process steps.
- Tabs or segmented control where comparison benefits users.
- Case-study cards and detail layout.
- Accordion FAQ.
- Consultation form.
- Footer with contact placeholders and legal links.
- Buttons: default, secondary, outline, ghost, link, destructive.
- Inputs, textarea, select, checkbox, radio, error summary.
- Toast/alert, dialog, tooltip, badge, skeleton, empty/error/success states.

## Prototype breakpoints

Provide representative screens at:

- 390px mobile.
- 768px tablet.
- 1440px desktop.

## Accessibility and quality

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Visible focus styles.
- Logical headings and landmarks.
- Keyboard-operable menus, dialogs, and accordions.
- 44px touch targets.
- Reduced-motion design.
- Realistic text lengths and no lorem ipsum.
- Images with stable aspect ratios and meaningful alt-text notes.

## Prototype deliverables

The first prototype is intentionally limited to four responsive pages:

1. Home.
2. Solutions overview.
3. Combined POS + MRA EIS solution detail.
4. Contact/consultation form with validation, pending, and success states.

Services, process, enterprise software, UX/UI, website development, full-stack development, and proof framing are represented as sections within those four pages.

Use placeholders for unverified contact, legal, partner, testimonial, and case-study details.
